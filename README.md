# 🌙 Mission Moonbase

**An interactive lunar outpost simulation game by Team The Moonies.**

Mission Moonbase is an educational simulation game designed to introduce young learners to the challenges of space exploration. Players take on the role of a lunar mission commander and manage a Moon base, making decisions that affect the survival of their crew.

The goal is simple: keep your crew alive while building a sustainable lunar outpost.

## 🚀 The Mission

Players must manage a lunar expedition with a crew of four for 30 mission days, working with limited supplies and resources.

Every decision comes with a trade-off. More solar panels provide additional power but consume cargo capacity. Stronger radiation shielding improves crew safety but requires additional resources. A greenhouse can produce food but also needs water and energy.

Can you keep the outpost running for all 30 days?

## 🎮 Core Features

- **Mission Setup:** Configure your lunar expedition and review mission objectives.
- **Outpost Construction:** Select and install equipment for your lunar base.
- **Resource Management:** Monitor oxygen, water, food, energy, and crew health.
- **Mission Simulation:** Advance through mission days and observe the effects of your decisions.
- **Random Events:** Respond to unexpected challenges such as equipment failures, dust accumulation, and solar storms.
- **Mission Debrief:** Review your performance, evaluate decisions, and learn from mission outcomes.

## 🛰️ Learning Objectives

Mission Moonbase introduces players to fundamental concepts in space exploration, including:

- Life-support systems
- Renewable energy and power management
- Radiation protection
- Food production in controlled environments
- Resource allocation and engineering trade-offs
- Decision-making under limited resources

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

The planned technology stack includes:

- **Frontend:** React.js with Vite
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
- **Animations:** Framer Motion
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Charts:** Recharts
- **Storage:** Browser LocalStorage
- **Simulation:** JavaScript-based simulation engine

## 🌌 Project Scope

The initial version focuses on a lunar outpost simulation. The resource models are simplified for educational gameplay and are not intended to represent certified engineering specifications for real space missions.
