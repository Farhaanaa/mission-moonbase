# 🌙 Mission Moonbase

### Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer

**Your mission. Your decisions. Your survival.**

Mission Moonbase is an interactive educational lunar survival simulation that introduces young learners to the scientific and engineering challenges of establishing a sustainable human presence on the Moon.

Players take command of a crew of four astronauts and manage a 30-day lunar expedition, making critical decisions about equipment, infrastructure and limited resources.

## 🚀 About the Project

Establishing a human settlement on the Moon requires more than reaching its surface. Astronauts must survive extreme temperatures, radiation exposure, limited resources and the challenges of maintaining essential life-support systems.

Mission Moonbase transforms these challenges into an interactive learning experience.

Players configure their mission, select equipment, construct a lunar outpost and manage essential resources while responding to unexpected events that can affect crew safety and mission success.

## 🎮 Key Features

- **Mission Briefing:** Explore mission objectives and understand the challenges of lunar exploration.
- **Science Primer:** Learn about lunar conditions, radiation, thermal cycles and In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU).
- **Mission Configuration:** Select mission parameters, landing locations and crew.
- **Equipment Selection:** Choose essential equipment while considering mission requirements and payload constraints.
- **Outpost Construction:** Configure a lunar base using selected equipment.
- **Resource Management:** Monitor oxygen, water, food and energy throughout the mission.
- **Dynamic Mission Events:** Respond to unexpected situations, including space-weather hazards.
- **Crew Monitoring:** Track astronaut conditions and mission status.
- **Mission Debrief:** Review mission performance and explore educational takeaways.

## 🌕 Learning Objectives

Mission Moonbase aims to help learners understand:

- The challenges of sustaining human life on the Moon.
- The importance of oxygen and water management.
- Radiation protection and space-weather hazards.
- Energy generation and storage.
- Food production in extraterrestrial environments.
- Equipment selection and engineering trade-offs.
- The role of ISRU in future lunar exploration.
